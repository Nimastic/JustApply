Cognition’s team values
01
It’s fun being on the Avengers
We care deeply about the work and the people behind it. We invest in each other, challenge each other, and bet on kind, low-ego individuals – showing up for one another and taking pride in what we build together.
02
Go for it all
We expand the frontier taking on hard problems and building what doesn’t yet exist, without letting constraints limit our ambition.
03
Correctness over comfort
We make the right call even when it gets messy. We disagree openly, assume positive intent and update our views when the evidence changes.
04
Everyone is GTM
We work alongside our customers – in real systems, under real constraints. We meet them where they are, going beyond tools to ensure they actually deliver value. We aim to transform the developer experience by staying in the work, not observing it from afar.
05
Just do things
When we see a problem, we solve it. We act with maximum agency and take full accountability for the outcome.
06
Every second counts
This is a moment that won’t wait, where the decisions being made now will define the next era of software engineering.
