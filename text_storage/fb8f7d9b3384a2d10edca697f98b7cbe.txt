Our Passion Shapes Our Culture
We see a world where robotics drives global progress, makes work safer, strengthens economies, and expands human potential. We're a team of multi-disciplinary, world-class professionals working every day to make that vision a reality, building a commercial company from our core in robotics research and development.

Employee Learning & Development
At Boston Dynamics, we are committed to the growth and development of our employees. Our training programs and initiatives provide a range of opportunities to help our people build new skills, expand their knowledge, and advance in their careers. Through tailored training programs, on-demand learning, expert speaker-led sessions, and networking events, we invest in our people to help them succeed. Whether you’re honing your technical abilities or developing new leadership skills, we provide the resources and opportunities to support your professional development every step of the way.
Events & Outreach
Boston Dynamics takes immense pride in our vibrant employee community and the meaningful impact we have on the community around us.
We actively foster a culture of inclusivity, ensuring that every employee feels valued and empowered to contribute.  By bringing together the amazing staff here, we make a tangible difference by engaging in a variety of celebrations, networking events, donation drives, and volunteer opportunities.
Dedicated to Inclusion and Belonging
We are committed to supporting Employee Resource Groups that inspire inclusivity and a sense of belonging in the workplace.
BD ERGs are voluntary, employee-led, employer-recognized groups that are open to all and align with the company’s mission. Our ERGs have made remarkable strides in organizing impactful events and delivering resources that cultivate a supportive work environment for all employees.
Boston Dynamics Internship Program
Boston Dynamics offers paid internship opportunities and developmental programs designed for talented and qualified students who are enrolled full-time and pursuing a bachelor’s degree, master’s degree, or PhD in technical or nontechnical fields.
These early career programs bring together diverse perspectives and provide invaluable hands-on industry experiences. Whether you’re searching for a 10 to 12-week internship or 6-month co-op opportunity, our programs offer meaningful experiences within the unique world of commercialized robotics.
