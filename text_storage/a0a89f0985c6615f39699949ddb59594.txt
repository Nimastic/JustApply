Your Questions Answered
Graduates
I am a student at university, am I eligible to apply for the Graduate Trader program?
Only penultimate and final year students are eligible to apply for the Graduate Trader program at Flow Traders. However, we encourage all students to come meet us at any of our upcoming events . All events can be found on our Career Events page.
When should I apply for the Graduate Software Development program?
We open applications for the Graduate Software Development program a year before the start date. Please be aware that we do not have a deadline for submitting applications, when all openings have been filled the position will be closed automatically.
What is the application deadline for the Graduate Trader program?
The Graduate Trader position is open all year round and we recruit students on a rolling basis. There are four intakes for this program per year.
What can I do to prepare for my Graduate Trader application?
Passion and motivation are essential elements to being successful in the Graduate Trader position. Below are some ways to learn more about trading that we share with students:
Start following what’s happening in the financial markets by keeping up with the news
Set up a mock or real trading portfolio or participate in trading competitions
Play games of statistics and strategy
Spend some time practicing your mental math skills
If you are keen on reading, check out some of these books:
Reminiscences of a Stock Operator by Edwin Lefevre
Market Wizards by Jack Schwager
Liar’s Poker by Michael Lewis
Specific to the products we trade, The ETF Handbook by David J. Abner or ETF For Dummies.
Does Flow Traders provide internship opportunities?
Yes, we provide internship opportunities within our Trading department in our Amsterdam and New York offices.
Does Flow Traders have experience with hiring talent from abroad?
Flow Traders has been hiring international talent for many years. We are very familiar with the relocation process for individuals and their families.
Am I required to come to a Flow Traders office during the recruitment process, if so will Flow Traders take care of my travel arrangements?
Yes, we aim to provide our candidates with the best experience. If you do not live in the location of your interview, our Recruitment Team will take care of planning your trip to our office, and will book your flights and accommodation.
Does Flow Traders provide relocation assistance?
This depends on the location you will be joining. Flow Traders will assist with various aspects (flights, visa application, accommodation at the start of your employment etc.).
Does Flow Traders provide visa sponsorship?
We are open to providing visa sponsorship to international candidates (and their partner/ children). We do not provide visa sponsorship for our interns.
How does Flow Traders support employees to continue to grow and learn?
We have a dedicated platform for continuous learning offering a variety of courses and trainings. We also sponsor relevant conferences throughout the year and encourage our employees to attend.
Am I able to work from a different office location during my career at Flow Traders?
Flow Traders offers a Global Rotation program open to employees across all regions. We believe sharing knowledge and global collaborations is crucial for success.
Is Flow Traders active on social media?
Yes, you can find us on LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter and YouTube. See the footer to direct you to our channels.
How long will my personal data be stored?
In line with GDPR regulations, we are permitted to store your data for up to 3 years after the date of your application. Our internal systems will automatically delete your data once this retention period has expired.

Technology
Why is technology important in trading?
We trade using proprietary and scalable software with many of our applications developed in-house. This includes pricing and trading software, market data processing tools, pre-trade risk controls, and other risk and compliance tools. One of our software platform’s core features is its modular design which allows us to rapidly test and implement ongoing enhancements.
How is Technology structured within Flow Traders?
As a technology-enabled trading firm our Technology department is at the core of our business and makes up over 40% of our global team.
What technologies are used at Flow Traders?
Our talented Software Engineers take advantage of the latest technologies to maintain and improve our cutting-edge trading environment. With a strong focus on code quality and maintainability, our C++ and Core Java Developers design and build the software suite that keeps Flow Traders ahead of our industry peers.
How are the new Software Engineers allocated over the different teams within Product Development?
During the interviews our Engineers will be looking at your technical strengths in combination with the business knowledge you possess. This will determine what team you will start your Flow Traders journey in. Please note we also want to hear from you and what you would be interested in working on!
Graduate Developers will experience working in a few different teams during the program. Based on your performance and ambitions you will decide together with your mentor which development team fits you best.
