FAQs
A new career is a huge undertaking and we know you’ll have questions. We’ve done our best to answer the most common queries here:
Is there career development?
IMC encourages continuous learning for employees at all levels. We offer several internal and external training programmes to encourage personal and skill development. Graduates begin their IMC career with an extensive Graduate Traineeship at one of our global office locations.
Will I be working solo or as part of a team?
We collaborate to succeed and celebrate our hard work through regular social events such as dinners, poker, movie nights, and Friday drinks. We also host an annual 'Weekend Trip' for colleagues each year.
What is working in the office like?
We take good care of you during working hours. You can enjoy a free catered breakfast, lunch, and dinner, choose from a wide variety of drinks, fruit, and snacks throughout the day, and enjoy a delicious, high-quality coffee made by our in-house barista.
Are there any health or well-being benefits?
IMC looks after its employees with subsidised gym memberships, specially designed desks, nutritious food and snacks provided daily, access to sports groups, and opportunities to get involved in giving and volunteering.
Are there any charity programmes?
Each year, IMC donates a share of our profits to non-profit organisations with a particular focus on education. Employees are encouraged to volunteer their time through a variety of opportunities and fundraisers. IMC also matches employee donations and provides grants to causes our employees are passionate about.
