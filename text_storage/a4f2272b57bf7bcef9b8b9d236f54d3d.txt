Trading
Decisive in Any Market
CTC traders combine proprietary models, deep market experience, and real-time judgment to form strong views – and act on them as conditions change.
What it looks like:
Pricing and inventorying risk across calm, volatile, and dislocated markets
Making split-second decisions and communicating them across the desk
Spotting opportunities, estimating their potential, and working with researchers and engineers to improve the strategy

Quant Research
Driving Alpha Through Research
CTC’s quantitative researchers use large, complex datasets to identify repeatable patterns, develop models, and turn rigorous research into strategies with measurable commercial impact.
What it looks like:
Distinguishing durable signals from noise across fast-moving market data
Developing pricing, forecasting, execution, or risk models
Working with traders and engineers to refine live strategies, measure the result, and constantly improve
Explore Quant Research at CTC
View Open Roles
Technology
Speed at Scale
CTC engineers create the high-performance systems, platforms, and tools that power trading and research across the firm.
What it looks like:
Architecting high-performance trading and research systems for fast, volatile, and high-volume markets
Making the right data easier to access, analyze, and act on across trading and research
Working side by side with traders and researchers, moving from validation of ideas to live market feedback with continuous iteration

Different Strengths. Shared Standards.
The people who thrive at CTC bring distinct expertise, but share a common approach to our work.
Rigorous Thinking
You examine the details, test assumptions, and hold your work to a high standard.
Curiosity
You ask thoughtful questions, seek out different perspectives, and keep learning.
Ownership
You take responsibility, follow work through, and step forward when something needs attention.
Collaboration
You share information, challenge ideas constructively, and contribute beyond your immediate role.
Low-Ego Leadership
You advocate for strong ideas, remain open to better ones, and put the outcome ahead of personal credit.
Grow with the Business
A career at CTC can develop in different ways. Deepen your expertise, broaden your understanding, and take on greater scope as your skills and judgment grow.
Learn
Build a strong understanding of your discipline and how every part of the business fits together.
Expand
Take on broader strategic challenges, work across teams, and help move strong ideas into practice.
Build
Apply your expertise to meaningful work, strengthen your judgment, and start solving more difficult problems.
Lead
Set direction, develop others, and shape how a team, strategy, or system evolves.
