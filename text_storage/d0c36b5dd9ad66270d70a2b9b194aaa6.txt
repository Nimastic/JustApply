Democratize access to robotics
Our team has a clear and audacious mission that drives how we work, act and make decisions together. To democratize access to robotics, we consistently build for others with curiosity, purpose and a sense of urgency.
Join us
Who we are
A global team at the forefront of applied AI and automation
We are a steadily growing, multidisciplinary team of professionals. At Intrinsic, you’ll collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the field who care about solving big, seemingly intractable problems. Our culture empowers individual autonomy and impact from day one.
Where we are
A global perspective
With labs and offices in Mountain View, Munich and Singapore, we are bringing together a global team of experts with different backgrounds, skillsets and perspectives. This helps us approach old problems in new ways, which is what we do every day. Learn more about our team,  and meet some of us below.

FAQ
More questions?
Does Intrinsic offer internships?
Yes, we have an internship program. Roles are usually posted at the beginning of the year. If a role is posted, we are currently accepting applications.
When will I hear back regarding my application?
If there is an interest in moving forward, a recruiter from Intrinsic will reach out to you.
A recruiter reached out to me from a company acquired by Intrinsic (Vicarious, Akasha, Open Source Robotics Corporation, etc.). Is this a legitimate point of contact for Intrinsic?
It is likely not legitimate. We are aware of several recruiting scams where people are posing as hiring managers for non-existent roles at Intrinsic. Please confirm the email address ends in @intrinsic.ai for anyone that reaches out to you.
