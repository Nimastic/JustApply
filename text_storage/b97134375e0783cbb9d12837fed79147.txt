Who we are
Our purpose is to help more and more people experience financial well-being, with clients ranging from governments, foundations and other large institutions to those investing on behalf of individuals – like firefighters, nurses, teachers and factory workers – saving for retirement. Clients turn to us – as both an asset manager and leading provider of financial technology – for the innovative solutions they need when planning for their most important goals.

Stay vigilant: Recruiting fraud
Your safety as a candidate is important to us. As you explore opportunities at BlackRock, please stay alert to any job posts or recruiter communications that may seem suspicious.
Keep in mind:
All roles we are recruiting for externally are posted on our careers site.
Recruiter emails will always come from an official
BlackRock.com
address and never from a webmail address (like Gmail or Yahoo). Recruiters will also never reach out or conduct interviews via chat or text. If a recruiter reaches out on LinkedIn, make sure it’s from a verified account.
BlackRock will never request payment, deposits, bank details, or other financial information at any time during our hiring process. We recommend that you do not disclose personal or financial information to anyone you do not know.
If you believe you’ve received fraudulent communication from someone claiming to represent BlackRock or our Talent Acquisition team, please contact
reportascam@blackrock.com
.
For more information and additional guidance, please visit
our page
on scam and fraud awareness.
