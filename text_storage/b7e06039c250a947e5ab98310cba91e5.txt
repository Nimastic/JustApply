Ownership meets partnership
Sharpen ideas together
At Balyasny, we believe great ideas emerge from the connections between people. Teams work together across the firm to solve problems and unlock insights.
Own the next challenge
We are a meritocracy, dedicated to giving outstanding performers the opportunity to grow and to lead. Balyasny's leadership has expanded from three founding partners to more than twenty today, including leaders from management, business departments, and top portfolio managers.
Access expertise
Our leaders build connections with team members at all levels. Accessibility to our Investment Committee, Management Committee, and Investment Team partners underpins the firm’s culture of curiosity, innovation, partnership, and smart risk-taking.
Deepen your skills
We provide employees with opportunities to build their careers and better serve our investors. Our development programs—such as Anthem, Bridger, and Leadership Foundations—advance careers at all stages to encourage long-term success.
