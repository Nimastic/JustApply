Opportunities for student and early careers
Opportunities for student and early careers
Our graduate, internship, and apprenticeship programmes are designed to nurture exceptional talent and shape the future of Man. We're seeking ambitious individuals with intellectual curiosity, energy, and the determination to thrive in our fast-paced, innovative environment. While academic excellence matters, we're equally interested in your unique perspective, problem-solving ability, and drive to make a meaningful impact in global financial markets. Join us to accelerate your career alongside world-class professionals in an environment where diverse thinking drives innovation.

Graduates
Our graduate programmes welcome high-calibre graduates from various degree disciplines to embark on transformative 18–24-month rotational schemes. We offer programmes across our business, from investment management and research to technology and operations, supporting you in developing the skills and expertise needed for a successful long-term career at Man. We run a thorough induction programme, where you will gain insights to departments all across the firm, improving your understanding of how our business works. We're looking for graduates with intellectual curiosity, adaptability, and the ambition to take on increasing responsibility as you discover where your unique talents can make the greatest impact.
What we offer you
A buddy from our NextGen working group to help comfortably embed you into the business
Access to our mentorship programme, where you'll be partnered with an experienced professional to help guide your career development
Opportunity to undertake professional qualifications such as CFA, IMC, ACCA whilst on the Graduate Programme.
Dedicated support from the programme lead
Internships
Our internship programmes welcome high-potential penultimate year university students from various educational backgrounds to join intensive 12-week placements across our business. From research to technology to responsible investment, you'll gain hands-on experience while developing the foundational skills needed to successfully kickstart a career in investment management. We're looking for interns with a keen desire to learn, proactive mindset, and the curiosity to explore how your academic knowledge translates into real-world impact at one of the world's leading investment management firms.
"During my internships at Man, I had the unique opportunity to design high-impact systems end to end, from database architecture to algorithm design. I was given the opportunity to learn new systems such as Clickhouse and ArcticDB, yielding a 275x improvement in throughput. Highlights of the internships were the impact of the projects, and incredible teams who would always find time to whiteboard me through the codebase."
William Sørensen
Intern, Technology, Man Group
What we offer you
A buddy from our NextGen working group to help comfortably embed you into the business
Additional activities throughout your internship to help improve your knowledge and skillset
Build your network with industry professionals with the potential for a return offer to our Graduate Programme
Dedicated support from the programme lead throughout the programme

Career returners
At Man Group, we understand that careers aren’t always linear. Our Career Returners Programme is designed to support individuals returning to the workforce after an extended break of at least 24 months. Whether you’ve stepped away from work to raise a family, provide care, or pursue other life priorities, we may have the perfect opportunity for you.
We know from experience that returning to work can feel daunting, so we have partnered with
Career Returners
to offer a 6-month returner programme to ease the transition. The programme consists of a series of professional coaching sessions, a mentor programme and a ready-made community of former and current career returners to support with navigating the path ahead.
"After a long career break, the Returnship programme has been incredible and truly energising. It is well-structured, offering valuable training and mentorship to support the transition back into work. On top of that, being surrounded by such an amazing and supportive team of sharp, driven, and inspiring individuals has been contagious in the best way!"
Victoria Ilyasova
Investment Analyst, External Alpha, Solutions, Man Group
