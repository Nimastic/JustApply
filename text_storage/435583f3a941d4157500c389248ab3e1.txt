Recruitment Fraud Awareness Notice
Recruitment Fraud Awareness Notice
Cerebras has become aware of fraudulent communications from individuals falsely claiming to be members of the Cerebras Systems Talent Acquisition team regarding employment opportunities. These fraudulent messages may contain enough accurate information to appear legitimate.
These messages are being sent by email from domains that do not belong to Cerebras and may claim that a job offer is being made in an attempt to collect personal or financial information from job seekers.
Please be aware:
Cerebras will only communicate with candidates using official company email addresses.
Cerebras posts open positions on this Careers page, which is the only official way to apply.
If you receive a suspicious communication claiming to be from Cerebras that does not come from an official Cerebras email address or domain, please do not respond, click links, open attachments, or provide personal information. Instead, verify opportunities or check your application status directly through this Careers page.
If you believe you have been targeted by a fraudulent recruiting communication impersonating Cerebras, please contact us at
security@cerebras.net
