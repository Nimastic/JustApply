Belonging at Verkada
At Verkada, we’re committed to making our workplace one where everyone feels a sense of belonging. We want to bring employees together in a place where conversations can flow freely and everyone can feel comfortable sharing their experiences. By creating Communities for like-minded peers, we hope to elevate all voices, raise awareness around issues that affect the employee experience, and create a healthier workplace. Here are a few of our thriving communities:
Women at Verkada
Connecting and empowering women at Verkada to lead, innovate, and succeed.

Interviewing at Verkada
We’re thrilled that you’re considering a role at Verkada. Our interview process is designed to help us get to know you, give you a clear picture of the role, and help you decide whether Verkada is the right place for you.
Initial screening
The interview process at Verkada typically begins with a phone or video call with a recruiter. This is a chance for us to learn about your background, skills and career goals while sharing more about Verkada’s mission, culture and values.
What to expect
Your recruiter will guide you through the role and answer any preliminary questions you may have.
Hiring manager screen
We believe it’s important for candidates to meet potential team members early in the interview process. During the Hiring Manager Screen, you’ll meet with the Hiring Manager or a potential team member to discuss role expectations, your qualifications, and how your experience aligns with the team’s goals.
What to expect
This conversation includes a mix of behavioral and role-specific questions, as well as insights into Verkada and our current projects.
Technical assessment
Depending on the role, candidates may be asked to complete technical assessments tailored to the position.
What to expect
These assessments are designed to evaluate your technical skills and problem-solving abilities relevant to the position. Your recruiter will provide you with information regarding your assessment, and will work with you to make sure you feel prepared.
On-site interviews
If it seems like the candidate is a good fit for the role, they’ll be invited for on-site interviews at one of Verkada’s offices.
What to expect
This step often includes meetings with team members, technical exercises, and discussions about your experience and how it aligns with Verkada’s needs. Your recruiter will guide you on what to expect during the visit, so you can put your best foot forward.
