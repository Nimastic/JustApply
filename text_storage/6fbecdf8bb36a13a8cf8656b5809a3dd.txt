Fraudulent Schemes Impersonating AQR Capital Management, LLC and Affiliates (collectively, "AQR")
Important Notice:
Fraudulent Schemes Impersonating AQR
More Info
There have been recent incidents of individuals and groups falsely claiming to be associated with AQR on social media platforms. These imposters have engaged in a variety of unauthorized conduct, including soliciting customers to carry out trading activities with them, impersonating AQR using the genuine names of AQR senior executives or employees to offer investment opportunities, claiming to offer investment training courses that are supposedly provided by AQR or claiming that AQR is backing certain initial coin offerings or is otherwise engaged in crypto related trading activity. There have also been fake websites and mobile applications that claim to be AQR (or associated with AQR) when in fact they are not. These are scams and not authorized by AQR in any way.
Please be aware that AQR does not engage in any investment activities, provide training or financial services, or conduct other regulated activities through social media platforms or messaging apps. Similarly, AQR, its executives and employees do  not contact prospects, clients or members of the public via social media platforms or messaging apps such as WhatsApp, Discord and Telegram to offer investment opportunities. AQR does not offer direct investments in cryptocurrencies or other digital assets, it does not provide a trading platform to members of the public, it does not have a mobile app, and it does not offer investment training courses to the public.
AQR and its partners, employees, directors, officers, advisors, successors, and agents are not responsible for any conduct by unauthorized parties and channels, nor are they responsible for the services or information provided by such unauthorized parties and channels. If you have any questions or wish to ascertain whether you are communicating with an authorized representative of AQR, please contact us at
https://www.aqr.com/Contact-Us
. If you believe that you or someone you know has been a victim of fraud, please report the matter to your local law enforcement or other relevant authorities. For India residents, you may lodge a report at the national cybercrime reporting portal (
https://cybercrime.gov.in
).

Connection and Community
Employees come together for traditions like Pub Quiz and Halloween Kids Day, and connect over ice-cream Wednesdays, food truck socials, and ping pong tournaments. We host Pickles n’ Slaw, a fun lunch-time interview series where employees can hear from senior leaders from throughout the firm. We also provide opportunities for our employees to volunteer together and offer one day off a year for employees to volunteer with an organization of their choice.
Benefits and Perks
AQR has a longstanding commitment to offering generous benefits to employees and their families, including 100% paid medical coverage. We also offer numerous perks including gym allowances, cell phone subsidies, stocked office pantries, free breakfasts and meal allowances.
Plugged into Greenwich, NYC, and the World
AQR’s headquarters is right next to the Greenwich train station, and a short walk to all of the restaurants and shops on lively Greenwich Avenue.
Our close proximity also puts everything New York City has to offer within easy reach. Many of our people live in Manhattan and commute together on Metro North. In addition to our headquarters in Connecticut, we also have offices in Bangalore, Dubai, Hong Kong, London, Munich, and Sydney.
