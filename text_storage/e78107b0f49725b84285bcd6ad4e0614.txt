Intersecting technology, research and entrepreneurship, Deep Tech companies’ ambitions are a reflection of the mammoth scientific and engineering challenges they seek to tackle.
We’ve journeyed with the community from our first day and are always on the lookout for diverse individuals with the grit, vision and talent to come on board with us to take on this mission.

Life at sginnovate
Life at SGInnovate
Explorers
Novel ideas and different perspectives excite us! Gain global exposure and work with innovators and entrepreneurs around the world in sectors from agrifood to space tech.
Inventors
Continuous learning creates fertile ground for innovative thinking. We invest in learning and development at all levels to help our team members reach their full potential.
Tinkerers
No two days are ever the same. Hone your problem-solving skills and find new ways to create value for the Deep Tech ecosystem.
Cheerleaders
We never go at it alone. Collaboration and teamwork make us stronger and help us perform better.
SGInnovate is committed to gender equality in Deep Tech
We are building a team that is as diverse as the Deep Tech community we serve - one in which each employee is empowered, engaged and given equal opportunities to thrive at every stage in their career.
