As a Kodiak AI intern, you’ll work shoulder-to-shoulder with the best.
As a Kodiak AI intern, you’ll work and learn alongside the AV industry’s top talent. The best way to jump start your career is to spend time with the best of the best in a supportive culture that values mentoring and growth.
Kodiak AI offers interns the ability to work on robotics projects that could soon be cruising on highways and through military missions. Our interns have the independence to design solutions for their projects and then work with their team members on how to integrate into the software stack.
Our internships last between 12 and 16 weeks. We have 8 interns per class working within different subject matter areas like Systems Engineering, Perception, Product, Hardware, Motion Planning & Controls, Simulation, and more. There are three intern classes per year:
Winter: January–April
Summer: May–August
Fall: September–December
