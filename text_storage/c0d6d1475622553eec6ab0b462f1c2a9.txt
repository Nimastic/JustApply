Understand the Interview Process.
1
Submit Your Application
Apply as early as possible - applications are reviewed
on a rolling basis
.
All you need to get started is your
most recent resume
.
2
Complete a Technical Challenge
Role-dependent:
Quantitative Trader roles
: Expect a
probability and logic
test.
Developer roles
: Expect a
coding challenge
focused on
fundamental tech concepts
.
At-home and timed
assessment.
3
Virtual First Round Interview
Conducted with a current
developer, quantitative trader, or researcher
.
Includes both
technical
and
behavioral
questions.
4
Final Round Interview
May be in-person or virtual, depending on the team.
A
half-day interview
involving hands-on practice and in-depth discussion of your skills and experience.
